About this translation

This book was generated with Wenyi.

Wenyi is an open-source AI translation tool for books and long-form writing across languages, including novels, social-science monographs, and narrative nonfiction. It offers a local desktop app, a command-line tool, and an optional self-hosted Web workspace.

Wenyi focuses on whole-book understanding and translation consistency. Whole-book prescanning, chapter digests, rolling context, and a real-time glossary support batch translation. Saved checkpoints, optional polishing, evidence-based whole-book AI review, visual proofreading, and monolingual or bilingual exports help make translations not just readable, but enjoyable to read.

The project continues to improve, and multilingual translation remains experimental. Quality depends on the chosen models, source text, and configuration; mistranslations, omissions, and inconsistent terminology or style may remain. This page describes project capabilities, not a guarantee that every stage was enabled or that this book was proofread by a person.
